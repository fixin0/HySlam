Usage:
  HySlam.exe --source:video --path <file> [options]
  HySlam.exe --load <bake-output-dir>
  HySlam.exe --verify <bake-output-dir>
  HySlam.exe --help

Options:
  --source:<type>      Frame source (supported: video). Default: video
  --path:<file>        Path to the video file (required unless --load or --verify)
  --calib:<file>       Optional camera calibration file (key=value)
  --dump:<directory>   Write metrics.csv, points.csv, trajectory.csv and summary.txt
  --load:<directory>   Open points.csv and trajectory.csv in the free-camera viewer
  --verify:<directory> Validate a bake output directory and exit (0 ok, 1 problems)
  --max-frames:<N>     Stop after reading N source frames (positive integer)
  --deterministic      Read one source frame per update instead of wall-clock pacing
  --loop-closure       Enable loop closure detection and drift correction (opt-in)
  --camera-height:<m>  Camera height above the road in meters. Default: 1.3
  --pitch:<deg>        Camera pitch override in degrees (positive looks down).
                       Default: derived from the road horizon (0.665 * frame height)
  --fov:<deg>          Horizontal field of view used by the fallback intrinsics
  --preset:<name>      Scene preset (see below). Default: road
  --min-depth:<m>      Near limit of the ground-plane depth window
  --max-depth:<m>      Far limit of the ground-plane depth window
  --structure-depth:<m> Maximum depth for triangulated structure
  --band-top:<f>       Top of the ground detection band, 0..1
  --band-bottom:<f>    Bottom of the ground detection band, 0..1
  --keyframe-frames:<N> Max processed frames per keyframe
  --max-step:<m>       Max camera step per processed frame
  --lk-window:<px>     Lucas-Kanade window size (odd, 5..61)
  --lk-levels:<N>      Lucas-Kanade pyramid levels (0..6)
  --lk-error:<e>       Max Lucas-Kanade per-point error for a valid match
  --bake               Headless multi-pass bake; implies --deterministic
  --bake-passes:<N>    Number of bake passes, 1..10. Default: 5
  --bake-output:<dir>  Bake output directory. Default: runs/bake_<timestamp>
  --bake-max-keyframes:<N> Keyframe history per bake pass, 8..8192. Default: 512
  --help, -h           Show this help

Options can also be separated from their values:
  --source video --path road.mp4 --calib camera.txt --dump out --max-frames 300

Scene presets (explicit options always override preset values):
  preset    depth (m)  structure  band        keyframe  step (m)  pitch   LK
  road      6 - 26     45         0.70-0.99   30        3.0       auto    21/3/20
  highway   10 - 55    120        0.68-0.86   12        10.0      auto    27/4/30
  fast      12 - 70    120        0.65-0.82   6         15.0      auto    31/4/35
  mountain  5 - 30     70         0.70-0.99   25        4.0       auto    21/3/25
  city      3 - 18     40         0.72-0.98   10        3.0       auto    21/3/20
  office    1.5 - 9    18         0.72-0.96   8         0.8       5 deg   21/3/20
  home      1.2 - 7    12         0.78-0.97   8         0.6       5 deg   21/3/20

  Aliases: indoor/house/room -> home, motorway/expressway -> highway,
  high-speed/highspeed/speed -> fast, urban -> city,
  mountain-road -> mountain. The LK column is window/levels/error.

  The preset must match the scene and the motion. 'fast' keeps the detection
  band just below the horizon so the larger inter-frame displacement still fits
  the LK search range; on slow footage it underestimates the path. 'home' and
  'office' assume a hand-held camera looking slightly down (pitch defaults to
  5 degrees) and a floor visible in the lower part of the frame.

  Motion prediction is always active. It forecasts the next camera step from the
  recent history and rejects tracking outliers that deviate too far, which helps
  on high-speed and low-texture footage. Structure points are triangulated from
  the last three keyframes (multi-keyframe), giving a denser map on complex
  city roads.

  Loop closure detection is opt-in (--loop-closure). When enabled, a keyframe
  whose ORB descriptors match an older keyframe snaps the trajectory back to that
  place. It helps routes that revisit locations but can misfire on forward-driving
  clips with repetitive road texture, so it is disabled by default.

Bake mode (--bake):
  Processes the whole video offline without opening the OpenGL window. The
  default 5-pass plan is: baseline, adaptive tracking, guided by the previous
  trajectory, structure completion, cleanup and final decision. Each pass is
  scored and only the best-scoring pass is exported. The progress bar reports
  the overall percentage and an ETA.

  Output files in --bake-output:
    points.csv, trajectory.csv, metrics.csv, passes.csv, summary.txt

  --bake implies --deterministic and replaces --dump. Bake options require
  --bake. Passes beyond five repeat the cleanup plan with a wider LK search.

  Example:
    HySlam.exe --source:video --path road.mp4 --preset:road --bake --bake-passes 5 --bake-output runs\bake_road

Read-back mode (--load / --verify):
  --load opens an exported map in the interactive free-camera viewer. No video is
  decoded and no tracking runs; points.csv and trajectory.csv are read back. The
  camera is framed on the whole map at startup. Left-drag orbits, the wheel zooms
  and Esc closes the viewer.

  --verify reads a bake output headlessly, cross-checks the CSV files against
  summary.txt and exits with code 0 when every check passes or 1 when a required
  file is missing, a CSV is malformed, or a count, path, span, metric row or
  selected pass disagrees. points.csv may contain the optional observations
  column; without it the confirmed-point count is only reported as a warning.

  --load and --verify are mutually exclusive. They cannot be combined with
  --bake, and --path/--dump are ignored in this mode.

  Examples:
    HySlam.exe --load runs\bake_road
    HySlam.exe --verify runs\bake_road

Metric scale:
  The planar odometry assumes the camera moves on a flat road and projects road
  features onto that plane. Distance is derived from --camera-height and the
  intrinsics (or --fov). When --pitch is omitted, the pitch is taken from the
  ground horizon measured at 0.665 of the frame height; a wrong horizon or camera
  height scales the whole trajectory. Use a real calibration file for the most
  accurate result.

Calibration file format (key=value, one per line, '#' or '//' for comments):
  fx, fy, cx, cy       Required pinhole intrinsics
  width, height        Optional resolution the intrinsics were measured at
  k1, k2, p1, p2, k3   Optional OpenCV radial/tangential distortion coefficients

Example camera.txt:
  fx=1050
  fy=1050
  cx=960
  cy=540
  width=1920
  height=1080
  k1=-0.12
  k2=0.08
  p1=0
  p2=0
  k3=0
